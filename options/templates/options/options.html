How this works (like a broker): buying pays the premium; selling (writing) credits the premium and blocks margin - an estimate of NSE's SPAN + exposure margin, released as you buy back. Zerodha's option charges are deducted on every order (₹20 brokerage, STT 0.15 % on sell premium, NSE 0.03553 %, SEBI, stamp duty, 18 % GST). NRML positions carry to expiry and are settled at intrinsic value against the underlying's closing price (STT 0.15 % on exercised ITM longs); MIS is squared off at 15:20. Stop loss / target are on the premium and checked every 15 s by the server - with this page closed too - exiting at the bid / ask. Limit orders fill when the bid / ask reaches your price; placed after hours they wait for 09:15 (like an AMO). Not modelled: physical delivery of stock options (cash-settled here), freeze-quantity slicing, peak-margin penalties.
