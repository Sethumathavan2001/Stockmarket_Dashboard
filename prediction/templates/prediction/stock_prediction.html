    Newer and stronger items count more (half-life 6 hours); company filings marked important count 1.5×. Each horizon's prediction is shifted by the news lean, sized to that horizon's normal range (up to 35 % of its half-width), and the range is widened on news-heavy days. The news adjustment is a rule of thumb - the app has no news history, so unlike the models it cannot be back-tested; Test accuracy, IC and Edge describe the model without news. Compare with the grey dashed line on the chart (the prediction without news).
    Model leaderboard - out-of-sample test
    Every model was trained only on the past and tested on the next, unseen period - four times (walk-forward). Accuracy = how often it called the direction right; Baseline = always calling the direction that turned out more common (a hard bar, known only afterwards). IC = correlation between predicted and actual move (above 0.03 and positive in most windows is a useful signal). Skill > 0 = smaller error than predicting "no move". Top 10 % = its most confident calls only. Avg net / trade = trading every prediction bigger than the cost, after costs (). Edge = IC ≥ 0.03 and positive in 3+ windows, beats the baseline, 30+ trades and profitable after costs.
